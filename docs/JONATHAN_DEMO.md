# Jonathan demo guide | Chugach Flow-Down Validator

**Audience:** Chugach Government Solutions stakeholder discussion. **Duration:** five minutes.

**Safety:** Use only public or synthetic text. Do not enter a real contract, proprietary scope, CUI, or controlled technical data. No outputs are legal determinations.

## Five-minute walkthrough

| Time | Action | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | Open the preview; identify scenario, scope and place of performance. | The proposed tool organizes prime-to-subcontract flow-down review around concrete contract context. |
| 0:45–1:45 | Click **Load synthetic review**. Show Include, Exclude, and Additional review cards, with source and rationale. | The goal is defensible decision support, not a black-box list of clauses. |
| 1:45–2:30 | Select a human determination, then use the category filters. | A responsible reviewer remains accountable and can accept or investigate each recommendation. |
| 2:30–3:30 | Paste `FAR 52.222-26 Equal Opportunity; DFARS 252.204-7012 Safeguarding Covered Defense Information.` and click **Extract candidate references**. | This is reference detection only; it does not infer applicability or require uploading text to a backend. |
| 3:30–4:30 | Click **Evaluate fictional rules**. Highlight the place-specific item in **Additional review**. | Unverified or research-dependent questions stay with a human rather than becoming automatic inclusions or exclusions. |
| 4:30–5:00 | Click **Export review CSV**, then explain the staged path toward an MVP. | The prototype shows the audit-friendly interaction. Accurate decisions will require approved sources, expert ground truth, and a secured deployment. |

## Suggested stakeholder questions

1. Which subcontract types and contract families should be the initial focus?
2. Can Chugach provide **sanitized or public** sample clauses, scope descriptions, and expert-reviewed target outcomes?
3. Who is the designated reviewer of candidate flow-down decisions, and what is the acceptable false-negative rate?
4. What environment, data classification, retention rules, and approval process would govern any later real-contract pilot?

## Honest boundaries

- All example classifications and rule sources are fictional.
- Public FAR/DFARS links are an unreviewed source registry, not an applicability engine.
- The browser performs local PDF/DOCX text extraction, but this is not a secure client-document ingestion system.
- There is no AI model integration, authenticated user workflow, persistent audit log, or validated legal conclusion.
- CSV contains the current page's reviewer selections and should be handled as a demonstration artifact.

## Go/no-go for next stage

Proceed to MVP scoping only after sample data and expert labeling are available, the governing security environment is approved, and measurable inclusion/over-inclusion and traceability targets are agreed.
